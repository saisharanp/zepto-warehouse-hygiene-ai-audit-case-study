# Stage 05 — Validation and Pilot Approval Gate

## Decision

**Proceed with preparation for offline evaluation and shadow-mode validation. Do not move to a live operational pilot or customer-facing hygiene badge yet.**

## Recommendation

Approve the validation protocol, synthetic replay cases, scorecard, and rollback runbook as the working Stage 05 package.

## Evidence

July 2026 reports describe recurring hygiene findings at multiple Zepto locations in Hyderabad, including food stored on floors, dirty surfaces, waste, pest evidence, weak segregation, missing records, and a temporary order halt at Narsingi. The evidence supports testing a more frequent, auditable control loop, but it does not provide Zepto’s internal baseline or prove that the proposed AI workflow works.

## Open questions

- What de-identified real inspection media can Zepto provide?
- Which checklist items need sensor data or physical sampling?
- What are the current inspection cadence, false-negative history, complaint baseline, and remediation SLAs?
- What legal and worker-notice basis will govern recording and retention?
- Who can authorize an affected-SKU hold, recall, or regulator escalation?

## Approval request

Approve collection of the required data and cross-functional sign-off for the four-week shadow-mode setup.
