# Stage 07 — Product Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this case study |
|---|---|
| Facility | Generic term for the operating site; the MVP scope is Zepto dark-store/warehouse operations. |
| Dark store | A customer-fulfilment facility not designed as a public retail store. |
| Verified Hygiene Check | The proposed risk-based live walkthrough, evidence-integrity, AI-triage, human-review, remediation, and re-check workflow. |
| AI triage | Model-assisted classification of visible evidence, quality, coverage, and uncertainty; not a regulatory verdict. |
| Pass recommendation | An AI recommendation that no critical issue is visible in the valid, complete scope; it does not release a hold until an authorised human decides. |
| Fail / hold | A workflow state that contains affected scope pending authorised review, remediation, physical checking, or escalation. |
| Unknown | Evidence is ambiguous, occluded, too poor, or incomplete to support a safe finding or pass recommendation. |
| Incomplete | Required zones, prompts, integrity signals, or evidence are missing; it is not a pass or a hygiene finding by itself. |
| Integrity exception | A mismatch or suspicious session signal such as replay, mock location, QR/NFC mismatch, or tampering. |
| Verified-control store-day | A store-day with complete location-verified evidence, no unresolved critical exception, and completed remediation/re-check where required. |
| Physical audit | Independent on-site inspection or sampling used for regulation, calibration, and challenge; it is not replaced by the virtual check. |
| RICE | Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort; a prioritization method, not an outcome metric. |
| Illustrative | A planning example used to show how the product or model works; not a Zepto live metric or result. |
